### Folded from Phases 0 to 4 (summarized 2026-11-13 and 2026-11-14)

- **Rulings and toolchain:** `session.canWrite` comes from `api/me` and hides write controls when false (OQ20). Dev server on 4200 (OQ12). Signal Forms on Angular 22.2.x (OQ17). Dirty is by reference, never `JSON.stringify`. Signal Forms: `[formField]`; custom controls must not declare inputs `min`, `max`, `name` or `disabled`.
- **Validator and wire:** `walkShell` in `engine/model/normalize.ts`; `validate.ts` maps adjustments 1:1 (`defaulted`→`missing`, `clamped`→`out-of-range`, `rounded`→`not-integer`, `truncated`→`too-long`, `dropped`→`unknown-property`, `cleaned`→`name-format`). `ShellIssue.code` is contract (schema.md §6.3). The Write tool turns `\uXXXX` and `“` into literal characters: write escapes via Python and grep for non-ASCII. Prettier reflows lines, so a mutant's `old` string comes from the formatted file.

- **Banner (point 12):** `core/api-status.ts` injects `SessionService`. `bannerStatus` is `unavailable` if the `/health/live` probe failed or `api/me` failed, `checking` while either is in flight, and hosts hide a first `checking`. `retry()` re-probes and reloads the session. `status` stays probe-only for `/status`. Spec traps: the designer spec never loads the session; a builder retry re-triggers the My shells LIST fetch (flush it or `http.verify()` fails); `settleSession(http, 'pending')` orders answers.
- **Undo (point 12):** `FORM_FIELDS` replaces `TEXT_FIELDS` and adds `select`, `input[type=range]` (inspector sliders, scrub bar) and `input[type=checkbox]` (Own burst height, Reduce flashing). Buttons and the timeline still undo the show. The N6 spec in `show-builder-page.spec.ts` covers them, with a button as the negative control.
- **Seek (point 13):** `FireworksStageComponent.seekSlice` loops `TimelineDriver.seek(loop, target, chunk)` until it arrives or the budget passes on `STAGE_CLOCK` (`InjectionToken`, default `performance.now()`). The first chunk is 1 step; later chunks are sized from the measured step cost, at most `SEEK_CHUNK_MAX_STEPS` (32). The driver is unchanged. Measure on Metal with `GPU=1` (`scratchpad/pw/luigi-f1.mjs`); SwiftShader's render swamps a small budget. At 25 ms (default): Done-when scrub 1.4 s, max show 183 s, frames p50 33 ms, Pause at 120 ms or under (8 ms was 3.3 s and 369 s).
- **Client config (point 13):** `core/client-config.ts` holds values only (`ClientConfigStore`, `DEFAULT_PLAYBACK`, `PLAYBACK_LIMITS`, `clampPlayback`: finite numbers clamp to 4–100, 0–5000, 1–60, 8–512, anything else takes the default; `checkpointOptions`). `core/client-config-loader.ts` (`ClientConfigLoader`) is the only HTTP: `SUPPRESS_ERROR_TOAST`, a second non-blocking `provideAppInitializer` in `app.config.ts`, defaults kept silently on failure. Readers use the store untracked; drivers read it at construction, so a late config never swaps a playing driver. `TimelineDriver.inherit` copies `from.cpSteps`: a new interval applies only to a driver built without `from`, and the byte cap applies on the next edit.

## Learnings

### 2026-11-15 — Post-gate cross-agent update (Scribe)

- **Your point 13's 8 ms is superseded in part.** The default seek budget is 25 ms, configurable through appsettings `ClientPlayback` (Bowser's record). The record is marked in place.
- **Toad's guard specs now pin your code.** `unsafe-sinks.spec.ts` allows only the shapes in `SHIPPED_URL_BINDINGS` (the two `/mine` card `routerLink`s) and pins 8 navigation calls. `designer-structure.spec.ts` has `showEntryViolations()`. A new URL binding or navigation call updates the ledger in the same change, with a reason.
- **Toad's contract spec reads your TS against C#.** `core/client-config.contract.spec.ts` checks `PLAYBACK_LIMITS` against the `[Range]` attributes and the committed appsettings. Keep `PLAYBACK_LIMITS` in step with any range change.
