## Learnings

### 2026-11-16 — Final tier, F1 floor, Boo F6, first full daily (record `toad-interlude-final-tier.md`)

- **Tiers:** `run.mjs final` = daily + `test:final` + `at-maximums` fingerprints in 3 engines + bench + gate-warm + `docker build --no-cache`. Final-only browser steps are in a `tier === 'final'` block in `tools/browser-gates/daily-steps.mjs` (`daily.mjs` takes `TIER=final`). `FINAL_ONLY_CASES` selects by name because golden `case` JSON carries `tier`. `daily-status.mjs` reads `daily/` and `final/`.
- **First full daily:** PASS in 21.4 min (load 3.9 at the start, 5.1 at the end, 5-min average about 9 late in the run). Regular part 77 s, `test:daily` 105 s, Chromium gates 10.9 min (keyboard-neg-focus 95 s and roundtrip-neg-ended 104 s are the slowest controls), Firefox/WebKit 6.4 min, fingerprints 62 s.
- **Report-only notes, both gate-script problems:** (1) `gate-keyboard.mjs:471` grants `clipboard-read`/`clipboard-write` unconditionally; Firefox and WebKit reject them, so the "mine" walk can't run. `gate-roundtrip.mjs:122` already guards this to Chromium only. (2) WebKit on macOS moves Tab only between text fields and selects; links and buttons need Option+Tab, so the walk lands on `body`. (3) Firefox makes the scrollable `aside` panels (`designer-panel`, `builder-panel`) tab stops before the skip link. This is engine behavior; whether the panel needs `tabindex="-1"` or the gate should expect it is a question for Rosalina and Luigi. Fixing the script is mine, and was not done in this batch.
- **F1:** the regular property seed never produced resize-and-back; F1a/F1b survived the property file. A directed key back (0.8 after mixed frames) plus the floor `key back`/`seek after key back` fixed it. Fast screen: `scratchpad/toad-f1cov/` (`entry.ts`, `mrun.sh <old> <new> <resizeBack>`).
- **F03:** KILLED (2 autosave tests) by `shell-designer.store.spec.ts`, 19 s, load 3.1.
- **Lesson:** check a coverage key's actual count before you trust its floor. "On by default" is not "generated".

### 2026-11-16: keyboard gate in all three engines
- `gate-keyboard.mjs`: added a module `ENGINE`, `tabKey()` (`Alt+Tab` in WebKit), `ENGINE_STOPS` plus `note()` for Firefox's scrollable `aside` stops, and a Chromium-only clipboard grant. All three engines pass 79/79; the Chromium negative controls are caught (walk 45/45, focus 23/23).

### 2026-11-16: Phase 8 B3 auth gates and pre-gate daily (record `toad-phase8-auth-gates.md`)
- **`tools/browser-gates/gate-auth.mjs`** (PARTS csrf, csp, walk; NEG for each). The positive run is the smoke step `auth` (about 30 s); daily adds `auth-neg-csrf|csp|walk`. `proxy-config.contract.spec.ts` covers F2. `run.mjs` now has `postgres: true` and `swaps` (exit 3 means some mutants were SKIPPED).
- **Stub:** `POST /__next-subject?sub=` gives a one-shot subject, so you can sign in as two accounts through the real flow.
- **Traps measured:** (1) In Chrome 157, a `route.fulfill` attacker page has its loopback fetches blocked by Local Network Access before they reach the server, which is a false CSRF pass. Use a real loopback server. (2) Playwright `allHeaders()` omits `Sec-Fetch-*`, and CORP- or CORS-blocked responses never fire `response`. Use CDP ExtraInfo events. (3) A launcher that hosts the in-process stub must not `spawnSync` the gate, because that deadlocks sign-in. (4) The PUT envelope is `{ document }` only; an extra `name` gives 400 before the owner-filtered 404. (5) A spec with `declare const process` needs `export {}`, or it collides with `designer-structure.spec.ts` in the `ng test` build. Typecheck alone passed it.
- **First pre-gate daily caught gate-script debt:** the keyboard `/mine` and K9 parts and the touch `/mine` part never signed in after Phase 8. They now sign in as `SAVE_SUBJECT`. WebKit stores no Secure cookie on http, so `roundtrip-webkit` plays `codes.show` with `JSON_ENGINE=chromium`, and `keyboard-webkit` notes the skips. Lesson: after an auth change, grep every gate for the pages that became session-dependent.
- **Pre-gate daily:** PASS, 21.7 min (`test-results/daily/2026-11-17T063525`, load 2.8 to 4.3), 0 report-only notes, `daily-status` OK. An earlier attempt ran with the lid closed (clamshell sleep at 22:03). It took 507 min, and 7 steps "timed out" at many times their limits plus one flaky reduced-motion check. Run the daily under `caffeinate -dims` with the lid open. A step time far above its own timeout means the machine slept.
