- **Outcome:** Proposed, then ruled by Skyler (rulings in the plan status line and index row 92). Findings: at the 40k cap at DPR 2 the main thread is 99.9% busy with frame p95 33 ms, and about 60% of that is render-side JS. The `pixi.js/unsafe-eval` polyfill costs about 2× at 40k, and making `uvs` and `rotation` static brings it to today's cost, so no eval is needed. An idle stage costs 20–25% of a core because it renders every frame. Recommended: render-on-change, then a render-only governor gated on a trigger, never on `starBudget` or capacity; a `WarmUpDelayMs` debounce; and an optional skip for short shows.
