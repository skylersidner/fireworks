# Luigi: Phase 6 Show Builder, as built

**Date:** 2026-11-14 · **Status:** proposed · **Phase:** 6 (build batch) · **Inputs:** plan Phase 6 (and its rulings), `docs/fireworks-app/design/phase-6.md`, `acceptance/phase-6.md`, `yoshi-phase6-timeline-driver.md`, schema.md §4, §5, §11, §13.

## What
1. **Route and files.** `/show` (lazy) is `features/show/pages/show-builder-page/` with three page-provided injectables: `ShowBuilderStore` (no HTTP, no identity), `ShowSaveFlow` (Save and Share) and `ShowLibraryFlow` ("My shells"). Pure helpers are in `features/show/shared/` (`show-edit.ts`, `show-draft.ts`, `show-copy.ts`, `timeline/`), and `ShowWorkingCopyHandoff` carries a `/c/` show to `/show`.
2. **The 2c.8 rule covers the builder.** `designer-structure.spec.ts` walks `features/show` too. Only `show-save.ts` and `show-library.ts` may reach `core/creations`, `core/session`, `core/creation-save` or `core/open-creation`. The store, the page, the draft, the edits and the handoff reach none of them. Only the page and `show-save.ts` inject the store.
3. **One edit path and Undo.** The store's `show` signal is replaced on every edit and never mutated. `edit(next, coalesce)` records Undo (50 steps, session only) and sets `dirty`. Edits with the same coalesce key in a row are one step (typing a name, dragging a slider, Duration). Undo and Redo are ⌘/Ctrl+Z and Shift+⌘/Ctrl+Z on the page, plus toolbar buttons. **Inside a text input or textarea the keys are left to the field** (Peach N6), so the field undoes its own text.
4. **Stage timeline mode.** `app-fireworks-stage` gains these:
   - `timeline` (a `TimelineDriver`) and `frameHook` inputs;
   - `skyClick`, `ended` and `catchingUp` outputs;
   - `seek(step)`, `currentStep()` and `seeking()`.

   In timeline mode, Launch, Replay, `fireNow` and clicks never fire. A click emits `skyClick` (x as a width fraction), paused or playing. A seek runs for about `SEEK_BUDGET_MS` (8 ms) of each frame, at least one step (point 13; was 480 steps per frame), only while the renderer is up, so cues fire at the real width. `catchingUp` reports past 300 ms. A new driver (an edit, built `{ from: previous }`) is put at the old driver's step. Frames stop stepping at the end, and `ended` is emitted once per arrival.
5. **No signal per frame.** The builder's playhead line, time readout and scrub value are written through element refs from `frameHook`, as are the player's meta time and progress line. The only signal writes from the hook are one-offs: the exact end, once per driver, and a view-range overrun, at most 4 Hz. **A span the hook writes must have no Angular binding inside it.** Setting `textContent` replaces Angular's text node, and later bindings then update a detached node. That froze the builder's time readout at the old show's end during the run.
6. **Shows preview and play at `starBudget` 1** (coordinator default). **Motion blur is a device preference** (Skyler, 2026-11-14; replaces phase-6.md §10.1's "read the designer draft, session-only"). `DevicePreferencesService` holds it under `fireworks.motionBlur.v1` (a decimal string, clamped to 0..0.92; absent or unreadable means the 0.78 default and nothing stored), beside `fireworks.reduceFlashing.v1`. The designer, the builder and the player all render with it:
   - The designer's Display slider writes it, and only once the value differs from the device's, so a first visit stores nothing. The designer draft still mirrors `display.persistence`, so the draft format is unchanged.
   - `adoptLegacyMotionBlur` copies a designer draft's value only while the device has none. Both the designer store and the builder store call it, so someone who opens `/show` first still keeps their old value. The builder only reads the designer draft and never writes it (spec: the draft stays byte-identical after a builder change).
   - The player has no Motion blur control, but it rendered with the 0.78 default. It now renders with the device value and never writes it. It does not adopt from the draft; the designer or builder does that on the next visit.
7. **Reduce flashing flash spacing (§14.2, D5)** is `FlashSpacing` in `engine/render/visual-rules.ts`, used by `pixi-renderer.ts` on every surface. A flash is identified by its start step, `round((simTime - age) × 120)`, which survives swap-remove. Flashes that start on the same step are one flash in time, drawn or skipped together. Sim time moving backward resets the gate. It's renderer-only, so ledger, counts and end are identical (spec).
8. **`/c/` dispatch.** `openCreation` opens shows (`status: 'show'`, upgrade then normalize). `newer` and `invalid` carry `kind`, so `/c/` words them for a show. Kind-unknown failures use the neutral copy (§10.4). `showNotSupportedMessage` is deleted. "My shells" also adds through `openCreation`, so Boo's C4/C5 single-entry rule still holds (`unsafe-sinks.spec.ts` lists the library). C14 allows the literal `'/show'`.
9. **Save copy is noun-aware.** `save-copy.ts` takes `noun` (`shell` or `show`) and a `reasonOf`. Shows pass `showIssueReason`, which gives §15 labels: cues by launch time, shells by name, never a raw path. `save-bar.ts` gains `cannotSaveNote`: the empty-show note replaces the owner note, and the buttons stay visible but disabled.
10. **Builder fields use native controls plus `range-row`'s `value`/`valueChange`, not Signal Forms.** This departs from OQ17 for this page only. **Accepted by Skyler on 2026-11-14 for the Show Builder only**; other pages still use Signal Forms. Each field change is a command on an immutable document with Undo and coalescing, not a form model, and a Signal Form writing its model in place would bypass Undo and `dirty`.
11. **Timeline keys act on the latest selection.** Keys can arrive faster than change detection (End then ← in one task). The timeline remembers the selection it last emitted until its input catches up. Zoom is computed from the store's current view, not the lagging input. A headless run found both.

12. **Gate follow-up (Skyler's rulings, 2026-11-14).**
    - **The API banner follows `api/me` too**, in the designer and the builder. `ApiStatusService.bannerStatus` combines the `/health/live` probe with `SessionService.status()`: `unavailable` if the probe failed; otherwise `checking` while either is in flight; otherwise `unavailable` if `api/me` failed. Hosts still hide a first `checking`, so nothing shows while `api/me` loads (Phase 5 N1). `ApiStatusService.retry()` (the banner's Try again) re-probes and reloads the session, so the save bar returns on recovery. Copy is unchanged (designer sentence, `BUILDER_API_BANNER`); phase-6.md §19.2 B1 already names the `api/me` case. `status` (the probe alone) is unchanged for `/status`. The session read lives in core, so pages still have exactly their `import core/api-status` and `inject(ApiStatusService)` hits; `designer-structure.spec.ts` needed no allowlist change.
    - **⌘/Ctrl+Z stays with every native form control the builder uses**: text fields, textarea, `select`, `input[type=range]` (inspector sliders and the scrub bar) and `input[type=checkbox]` (Own burst height, the Reduce flashing switch). `FORM_FIELDS` in `show-builder-page.ts` replaces `TEXT_FIELDS`. Buttons and the timeline still undo the show. For Rosalina: phase-6.md §18 Q1 needs this wording.

13. **Seek is budgeted by time (Boo Phase 6 F1, Skyler approved 2026-11-15).** `FireworksStageComponent.seekSlice` calls `TimelineDriver.seek(loop, target, chunk)` in a loop until it arrives or `SEEK_BUDGET_MS` (8) has passed on `STAGE_CLOCK` (an `InjectionToken<() => number>`, default `performance.now()`; specs inject a fake). The first chunk is 1 step, so every frame advances. Later chunks are sized from the measured step cost to what is left, at most `SEEK_CHUNK_MAX_STEPS` (32) between clock reads, so a frame overshoots by about one step. **The driver is unchanged.** Repeated capped `seek` calls with the same target already equal one uncapped call, and the target is re-read every frame, so a new scrub target, Pause and edits act between frames. `SEEK_STEPS_PER_FRAME` is removed. `catchingUp` still waits 300 ms.
    - > Superseded in part by `bowser-phase6-client-playback-config.md` on 2026-11-15: the 8 ms budget named in point 4 and in this point is now 25 ms by default, configurable through appsettings `ClientPlayback` (`SeekBudgetMs`). The time-budgeted seek itself stands.
    - **Served config (Skyler, 2026-11-15, follow-up).** The budget and related knobs now come from the server's appsettings through `GET api/client-config` (`{ playback: { seekBudgetMs, catchingUpDelayMs, checkpointIntervalSeconds, checkpointMemoryMiB } }`, anonymous, no-store; Bowser's endpoint). `SEEK_BUDGET_MS` and `CATCHING_UP_DELAY_MS` are gone: the stage reads `ClientConfigStore.playback` (untracked) once per seek frame, so a value that arrives mid-seek applies from the next frame. **The default seek budget is 25 ms** (was 8), catching up 300 ms, checkpoints every 10 s (1200 steps) under 64 MiB, the same as the server's appsettings.
    - **Split for the 2c.8 rule.** `core/client-config.ts` is values only: `ClientConfigStore` (signal, defaults), `PLAYBACK_LIMITS`, `clampPlayback` (finite numbers clamp to 4–100, 0–5000, 1–60, 8–512; anything else, including numeric strings, takes the default) and `checkpointOptions` (`checkpointSteps = s * 120`, `checkpointBytes = MiB * 1024 * 1024`). `core/client-config-loader.ts` (`ClientConfigLoader`) does the GET with SUPPRESS_ERROR_TOAST from a second non-blocking `provideAppInitializer`; a failure keeps the defaults with no banner and no toast. The stage, the builder store and the player read only the store; the loader is in the structure spec's pinned HTTP-reaching list.
    - **Drivers.** The builder store and the player pass `checkpointOptions(untracked(playback))` to every `TimelineDriver` they build. Untracked on purpose: a config arriving late does not replace a driver mid-play. In the builder a later edit's driver takes the new memory cap, but the spacing is inherited through `from` (the driver keeps one checkpoint grid) until the page builds a driver without `from`.
    - **Measured at 25 ms (served by Bowser's endpoint on 5180, via the 4200 proxy; Chrome headless on the Metal GPU, load average 7 to 13).** Done-when show to the end: 1.4 s (was 3.3 s at 8 ms), 51 frames at p50 and max 33 to 34 ms, no long tasks. `at-maximums`: 183 s (was 369 s), frames p50 33 ms, p99 50 ms, max 217 ms, 30 long tasks (longest 114 ms), and a Pause click mid-seek took 461 ms (was 177 ms). So 25 ms halves the worst-case scrub at about 30 fps; the cost is responsiveness on the max show.
    - **Trade-off, measured on the dev server, Chrome headless on the Metal GPU:** a scrub to the end of the Done-when show (8,136 steps) took 1.5 s at 18 frames of about 83 ms with the old 480-step slice, and takes 3.3 s at a steady 17 ms per frame now. The `at-maximums` show (74,676 steps at about 40k particles) scrubbed to the end in 369 s. Frames ran at p50 17 ms, p90 33 ms and max 167 ms, the longest task was 133 ms, and a Pause click took 177 ms. Boo measured 142 s in SwiftShader with frames of 2 s each, so the end now arrives about 2.6 times later but the tab stays responsive. The load average was 9 to 18. Short re-seeks of about 10 s of show, about 150 ms of work, now cross 300 ms and briefly show "Catching up…". To trade back throughput, raise `SEEK_BUDGET_MS`; nothing else depends on it.
    - **Not built: background checkpoint warm-up** for a linked show. Moved to Phase 7. It needs a second sim stepping at the same width, star budget and capacity, and it competes with this budget for the same frame time, so it is not cheap. Its exactness rides on the driver's checkpoint key, which is good, but it needs its own review. **Superseded in part by `luigi-phase7-client-hardening.md` (point 5) on 2026-11-15: warm-up is built in Phase 7**, because Skyler ruled that cold seeks count toward the 1 s trigger.

## Why
- Point 13: a fixed step count is unbounded in time. At the 40k cap one step costs 2–4 ms, so 480 steps froze the tab for 1–2 s per frame. Point 13 keeps the stage, not the engine, in charge of wall time, so the engine stays clock-free.
- Points 2 and 8 keep Phase 5's security and structure rules true for the new surface instead of adding exceptions to them.
- Points 4 and 5 meet the standing "never write a signal on every frame" rule with live playback that's 100% DOM-driven.
- Point 10: Undo across the inspector, the panel and the timeline needs one edit path. With Signal Forms there would be two writers.
